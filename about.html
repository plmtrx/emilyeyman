Hi, I’m Emily Eyman, a Los Angeles-based photographer and filmmaker who loves capturing the little moments that feel like you.

Originally from Santa Barbara, my relationship with the camera started at 12 years old when my dad gifted me my first DSLR. What began as a childhood passion eventually grew into something much bigger. After studying film in college and working on behind-the-scenes projects for music videos and short films, I turned my passion for photography and filmmaking into a career.

I specialize in portraits, lifestyle photography, and video, creating imagery that feels natural, cinematic, and authentic. My goal is to help you feel completely yourself while capturing the in-between moments, movement, and details that make your memories feel alive.
